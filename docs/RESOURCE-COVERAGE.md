## Items flagged for a human check

- SBA loan portal (lending.sba.gov) and the COVID EIDL / MySBA phone numbers — marked Needs verification.
- My Alabama Taxes URL came from ADOR pages and PDFs rather than a direct search result.
- Birmingham online tax portal (HdL) and Jefferson County online services (srt.jccal.org) — confirm before relying on them.
- Alabama beer tax: sources disagree on whether ADOR or the ABC Board collects it.
- Birmingham sales tax: confirm whether filing is through the city's HdL portal or My Alabama Taxes.

## How to finish coverage

Research for the remaining states needs another pass (the research search budget ran out, and .gov sites can't be opened from the build sandbox). Add rows from the staff Resource Center page, or update `data/resource-research/*.json` and run `python3 scripts/build-resource-library.py` to regenerate the library migration.
